Think like a CEO

You’re the CEO, the manager and the employee. That’s the problem.

Who’s running your business while you’re busy working in it? Every business needs three roles. When you’re starting out, you’re all three.

Lives in the present

The Technician

Does the work. Makes the product, delivers the service, solves today’s problems.

Protects the process

The Manager

Creates order, organizes resources and makes sure the work gets done right, every time.

Builds the future

The CEO

Drives the vision, spots opportunities, sets the growth strategy and decides where the company goes.

We help you think like an owner, build like an operator and lead like a CEO. The goal isn’t to create yourself another job. It’s to build a business.
