Step one

Put AF Alerts on your phone

Install AF Alerts so it opens like an app. On iPhone this is required: Apple only delivers alerts to apps on the home screen.

  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  3. Open AF Alerts from your home screen, sign in, and tap Turn On Alerts.

On your phone, open adventurefuel.agency/alerts in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
